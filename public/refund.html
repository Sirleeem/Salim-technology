Refund Policy

Last updated: 24 August 2026

1. Scope

This policy applies to payments made to SALIM TECHNOLOGY (BN 9760415) for software development and related technology Services.

2. Deposits and milestones

Project deposits secure your slot in our schedule and cover initial planning and design work. Deposits are refundable in full if you cancel before work has started. Once work has started, deposits are refundable on a pro-rata basis minus the value of work already completed.

3. Completed work

Fees for completed and accepted deliverables are non-refundable. If a deliverable does not match the agreed specification, we will fix it at no additional cost.

4. How to request

Email hello@salimtechnology.com with your project name and reason for the request. We respond within 24 hours during business days (Mon–Sat, 9:00–18:00 WAT). Approved refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5–10 business days.
