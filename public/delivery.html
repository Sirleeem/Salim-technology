Delivery & Fulfillment Policy

Last updated: 24 August 2026

1. Digital delivery

All Services and deliverables are provided digitally — no physical goods are shipped. Deliverables include source code repositories, hosted applications, documentation, and configuration access.

2. Timelines

Estimated delivery timelines are stated in each project agreement before work begins. Typical engagements range from a few days (small sites/fixes) to several weeks (full applications). We notify you promptly of any expected delay.

3. Acceptance

You will receive deliverables for review. You have 7 business days to test and request corrections against the agreed specification. After acceptance or the review window, the deliverable is considered fulfilled.

4. Support after delivery

We provide reasonable post-delivery support for defects at no charge for 14 days. Ongoing maintenance is available as a separate agreement.

5. Order inquiries

We commit to responding to order or project inquiries within 24 hours during business days (Mon–Sat, 9:00–18:00 WAT).
